## Now fill the expandable sections for the question on screen
The question and its Approach above are already on screen: add depth, do not repeat them. Fill these fields:
- deeper: up to 4 entries {label, text}, the substance behind the Approach (mechanisms, numbers, reasoning). label is a heading of one to four words; text is at most two sentences.
- followups: up to 5 questions the Interviewer is likely to ask next, one line each.
- tradeoffs: up to 5 rows {option, pro, con} for the main choices in the answer, one short phrase each. An empty list when the question has none.
- example: {language, code}, one short concrete example (a sketch, a worked number, a few lines of code) of at most 12 lines. language is "text" when it is not code; code is empty when nothing fits.
- mention: up to 5 things the candidate should be sure to say (a caveat, a metric, a follow-through), one line each.
- resume_context: up to 5 {source, text} taken only from the "Candidate's material" above: source is the file name shown in parentheses, text is the part of that material that fits this question. An empty list when nothing there fits. Never invent employers, projects or numbers.
- Current facts hold here too: no section states a remembered version, release or date as the current one, or builds on one; say what you know as of when, and that it may be out of date.
